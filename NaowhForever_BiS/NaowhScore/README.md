# Naowh Score

One number for a character's gear, on the item level scale: 26.4 means the gear is worth a
set of level 26 epics. Ours, worked out from the gear itself, so it reads the same for every
player, whether or not they run Naowh Forever.

## The formula

Each item counts as the item level of an epic with the same stats:
`level x SCALE[quality] + SHIFT[quality]` (an epic its own level, a blue about 0.8 x, a green
about 0.67 x - 5, never below 0). Each slot weighs that by how many stats it carries
(`Score.SLOTS`, chest = 1: head and legs about 1, shoulders, hands, waist and feet about 0.75,
neck, wrists, rings, cloak and off hand about 0.55, trinkets 0.7, main hand about 0.41, ranged
about 0.31). The score is the weighted average over every gear slot (not the shirt or
tabard): an empty slot counts 0, and a two-hander takes both hands' weights. A full set of
level L epics scores L.

## Its constants

`SLOTS`, `SCALE` and `SHIFT` live in `Data/Formula.lua`, which is generated: never edit it by
hand. `Tools/build/fit_naowh_score.py` fits them to a Forever build's own item table (wago.tools):
each item's stat budget (its stats, each at a fitted cost, primary stats costing 1) against
its slot, quality and level. Slot weights are the fitted slot multipliers; each quality's line
is its budget in epic item levels. Grey and white items carry no stats, so they take the
green line times their weapon DPS share of a green's. Trinkets keep 0.7, a judgement call:
their power is mostly in effects no stat shows. Legendary, artifact and heirloom count as epic.
Per item the fit explains about 96% of the budget's spread; on simulated gear sets the first
fit's typical error against the true epic level was about 1 level, with no class favoured on
the BiS lists' sets.

The daily watch (`.github/workflows/daily-watch.yml`) refits them for every new Forever build,
in the same pull request that moves the data to it, with a "Naowh Score" section in its
report: old against new, the fit's quality and its gates. `Formula.lua` is rewritten only when
the fit passes its gates (a full epic set of level L scores L, R^2 at least 0.90, slots in a
sensible order, grey < white < green < blue < epic) and something moves enough: a slot by
0.02, or a quality's worth at item level 60 by 0.5. By hand:

```sh
python Tools/build/fit_naowh_score.py           # report only, for wago.BUILD
python Tools/build/fit_naowh_score.py --write   # and rewrite Formula.lua when it should
```

`--build` picks another build, `--cache DIR` keeps the tables it reads (outside the repo).

## Its colour

A score takes an item quality's colour by its share of the best there is (`Score.Best`): grey
under 25%, white, green from 45%, blue from 65%, purple from 80%, orange from 95%. The best is
worked out from the gear the addon knows (Shared's ItemFacts: dungeon loot and faction rewards):
each slot's best item, two different rings and trinkets, a two-hander or a main and off hand.
Grade Against (BiS List > Character > Naowh Score) picks the best in the game (anyone levelling is grey or
white) or the best the player's level can wear (who is well geared for where they are), the
default: graded against the whole game, a levelling character's well-chosen greens read as a
near-empty bar and looked broken to players. New
loot in the data raises the best with no number to keep.

## Where it shows

- The BiS List's paperdoll: yours now, then with your BiS, and the gain (`BiS/Gains.lua`).
- Player tooltips (BiS List > Character > Naowh Score, on by default): yours from what you wear; a Naowh
  Forever user's as they share it; anyone else's from their gear, inspected as the game's own
  Inspect does. Scan Your Group reads your group's in the background, so theirs are ready.

## Sharing

Running Naowh Forever means sharing your score: there is no switch. It goes to your group and
guild when it changes (once per gear swap), when you join a group, and when asked; you ask
your group when you join it, and your guild once a session. So a Naowh Forever user's score is
there at once, at any distance, and always current. Nothing goes out in combat. Its cost is a
few events and a short message when your gear changes (measured in the test).

## Layout

```
NaowhScore.xml       load order
Data/Formula.lua     the formula's constants, generated by Tools/build/fit_naowh_score.py; makes ns.NaowhScore
NaowhScore.lua       the formula (ns.NaowhScore): a unit's score, a set of links, the best, the grade,
                     the colour and the text
Inspect.lua          others' scores: kept by GUID, on their tooltips, inspected one at a time,
                     your group walked in the background
Share.lua            yours sent to your group and guild, theirs kept; always on
UI/SettingsPage.lua  its card on BiS List > Character
```

## Why

- `Data/Formula.lua` loads first and makes `ns.NaowhScore`: it is data only, rewritten by the fit,
  so the formula in `NaowhScore.lua` hangs off the table it made.
- The gear slots are read main hand before off hand, so a two-hander is known first and takes the off
  hand's weight too. The shirt and tabard do not count.
- An item with no required level in the data is taken to need its item level less `LEVEL_GAP` (5).
- A level's best is a full set of blues `AHEAD` (5) item levels over it (dungeon blues are about five
  levels over what they need), or what the data offers that level if more, never over the best in the
  game. The data alone would not do: below the first dungeons it has nothing for most slots, and a
  level 12 in whites would read as the best there is.
- Rings and trinkets need two different items for the best; a two-hander or a main and off hand,
  whichever counts more.
- The colour ramp (`RAMP`, `{ share, r, g, b }` stops) is ours, so a little better reads a little
  brighter; the paperdoll's and the character panel's bars draw the same stops. With Both, the
  tooltip's share of their level's best is in gold, the colour of a level's goal on the cards.
- Inspecting: one request at a time, `INSPECT_GAP` apart (the server drops ones that come faster),
  waited on `WAIT_FOR` seconds, only in inspect range (`CheckInteractDistance` 1) and out of combat,
  and never while the game's Inspect window, the talents opened from it, or another request holds the
  one inspect the game keeps. Your own inspect wins: ours is dropped, so its answer can't clear yours,
  and ours waits `USER_WAIT` seconds while its window loads.
- An inspected score is kept `KEEP` seconds and goes stale when they change gear; a shared one is kept
  for the session and always wins. At most `MAX_KEPT` players are kept, the oldest going first.
- Guildmates' last scores are saved account-wide for the guild list's offline members: at most
  `SAVED_MAX`, dropped after `SAVED_DAYS` days.
- The walk looks again `RETRY` seconds later while one not known yet is out of range, and stops once
  none is left. The player you hover goes first, waiting at the front while the inspect is busy.
- The tooltip hook goes in a frame after Apply, once every module's Apply has run: tooltip post-calls
  run in the order they were added, so the badge line (Badges) comes first and the score under it,
  as one Naowh block. The label is in Naowh's blue without the logo, which stays with the badge line.
- Sharing: only while the Naowh Score and Share My Score are on; others' scores are kept either way.
  A score is sent in tenths, `S <GUID> <tenths> <level>`; a gear swap's burst sends once,
  `SEND_DELAY` after it; a request (`R`) goes to your group when you join it and to your guild once a
  session; each answer waits up to `ANSWER_SPREAD` tenths of a second at random so a raid's or a
  guild's do not all come at once, and the guild is answered at most every `GUILD_ANSWER_GAP` seconds.
  Nothing goes out in combat; it waits for combat's end. A score is kept only from the player its GUID
  names, found in your group or guild (`ns.SenderIs`).

## Checking

- `lua Tools/regression/test-naowh-score.lua`: the formula on known sets; the tooltips (off
  until turned on, one request at a time, kept, never in combat or while the game's Inspect
  window holds the inspect); the group scan (one at a time, skipping who shares, stopping
  when all are known); sharing (only with Share My Score and the score on, once per swap, held in
  combat, answered once); and
  each hot path's time and garbage.
- `python -m unittest Tools/tests/test_fit_naowh_score.py`: the fit on a made-up item table
  whose answer is known, its gates, the change gate and the Lua it writes.
- In game: hover yourself and a party member in range, with the setting on.
