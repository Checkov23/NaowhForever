# Training Planner

Every spell your class still has to learn: what you can train now, what each level brings and what
it costs, with the trainer's own prices once you have visited one. A road to 60 with a dot for every
level that brings spells, a Builds tab with talent builds level by level (Naowh's, saved, imported,
edited, followed as you level), a mini bar, a level-up toast, a panel beside your class trainer and a
Trainable tab under the spellbook.
Laid out like the [Dungeon Journal](../NaowhForever_DungeonJournal/README.md), on
[`Shared/`](../Shared/README.md).

## Layout

```
NaowhForever_Training/
  NaowhForever_Training.toc   metadata, and Training.xml
  Training.xml       every file, in load order
  Training.lua       settings (ns.TrainingSettings), the module table (ns.Training) and what it keeps
                     account-wide: prices, skipped spells, change listeners
  Constants.lua      the numbers several files share: max level, talent points, name length (Training.C)
  Data/
    TrainingData.lua   every spell each class learns as it levels, generated by Tools/build/training.py
    TrainingBuilds.lua each class's talent tree and the builds that ship, by hand
    TrainingWeapons.lua each class's weapon skills and who teaches them, generated by Tools/build/training_weapons.py
  Plan.lua           your class's spells by what stands in the way, and their totals (Training.Plan)
  Upgrades.lua       what a rank adds over the one before (Training.Compare, Training.Upgrade)
  Coins.lua          money as the planner writes it (Training.Coins)
  Route.lua          the nearest class trainer and the waypoint route through your professions' trainers
  Builds.lua         talent builds: check, edit, share, learn, follow
  Trainer.lua        the prices a class trainer asks, and the module's events
  View/
    Style.lua        the planner's own look, over Shared/Style.lua (Training.Style)
    Rows.lua         pooled cards, rows and section titles, a spell's tooltip and menu (Training.Rows)
    Spells.lua       the Spells tab: next visit, road to 60, spell lists (Training.Spells)
    Builds.lua       the Builds tab: builds, steps, the editor (Talents and Spells tabs) (Training.BuildsView)
  UI/
    Place.lua        windows on whole pixels, kept account-wide
    Window.lua       the window (/nftraining, ns.OpenTrainingWindow, ns.ToggleTrainingWindow)
    MiniBar.lua      the mini bar
    Toast.lua        the Level-Up Toast
    TrainerPanel.lua the Panel at the Trainer
    Spellbook.lua    the Trainable tab under the spellbook and its panel
    SettingsPage.lua its settings page (Training Planner/Settings), declared as cards
```

`Training.View` is the window's state (its frames, the tab, the level and build shown) that the
view files and the window share.

## Why

- The on/off switches' defaults (`enabled`, `levelUpToast`, `trainerPanel`, `spellbook`) are read from
  `ns.FEATURES.training`; the Trainer Popup card's switch belongs to the QoL store, and QoL's `Questing/Trainer.lua` declares
  that card on this page, so it goes when QoL is off.
- TrainingData rows are `{ level, spellID, cost, flags }` in level order; cost is the trainer's base
  price in copper. Flags: `quest` (taught by a quest), `needs` (the rank to learn first), `talent`
  (the talent it needs), `races` (the races that learn it). The trainer window updates a price to
  what it actually asked, kept account-wide.
- Weapon skills are not in TrainingData: Wowhead lists them at level 0 with no price and no trainer, and
  the class trainer does not teach them. The weapon masters of the capitals do, so TrainingWeapons rows
  are `{ level, spellID, 0, masters = { names } }`, the names being the Weapon Master NPCs of
  `Shared/Game/Towns.lua`. The level is the game's own MinLevel for the class (Polearms, Dual Wield),
  else 1; Dual Wield has no master (the class trainer teaches it). The Weapon Skills section of the
  Spells tab lists the ones you lack, and a click puts a waypoint on the nearest master of your faction.
  No price is known, so they read "At the trainer".
- A talent node is `{ spell, ranks, row, column, slot in the row, the node it needs at full rank }`; a
  build's points are nodes, one per point from level 10, 51 in all, 5 in a column for each row above.
- A talent's spell shows its full rank's text, so its tooltip reads the text of its rank and the next from
  the node's trait entry (GetTraitEntry), as the game's talent frame does. Another class's nodes are not in
  your talent config, so their tooltip shows the spell.
- A rank counts as learned once a later rank is: some ranks replace the one before. A rank before
  that no trainer sells (granted at level 1) is not in the data, so the game is asked. PLAYER_LEVEL_UP
  says the new level before UnitLevel does, so the toast plans for the level it was given.
- The trainer's list has names and required levels but no spell IDs, so a service is the class spell
  of that name and level; a header has no level and matches nothing. A pet trainer shares the class
  trainer's window but none of its services is a class spell. Forever's service kind can be missing
  (seen at profession trainers); then it is offered once its level is reached.
- Learning goes from the last index first: buying a service can renumber the ones after it, and one
  whose index now names another service is skipped.
- What a rank adds is the first number in its description that grew and is not a time or a distance,
  named by up to two words after it (or before it); two descriptions that do not line up show none.
  Descriptions the client has not loaded are asked for, and arrive as SPELL_DATA_LOAD_RESULT; a burst
  of them draws once, 0.1s later.
- The nearest trainer is your class's for your faction on your continent; with none there, or no
  position (in an instance), one in your faction's capital, then a shared one.
- An imported build is data, never run: its class must have a tree here, its points must pass the
  rules, and its name loses escape codes and line breaks and is cut to 40 letters.
- A build is kept by a number of its own, given the first time it is asked for, or a built-in one by
  its place in the data: names repeat, so they cannot be keys. Committing talents fires the talent
  events that start a followed build's learning again, so learning is guarded while it runs.
- A saved build can carry spells to skip (`skip`, spell IDs by rank, set in the editor, any spell of the
  class whether learned or not). The build you follow decides what Learn Selected at the trainer leaves out
  and what the Spells tab calls skipped, next to the character's own right-click skips. Those own skips
  are kept per character, so they never reach a new one; the build's are account-wide, travel in a
  build's export string and the Profiles share, and a new character gets them by following the build.
- The editor's Spells tab lists a spell with more than one rank on a row of its own, which skips every
  rank, then each rank on its own row. A skip has always been kept by rank, the whole-spell row writing
  every rank's ID, so builds saved or shared before skips by rank still skip every rank.
- Coins are written as text, each coin's letter in its colour: the game's coin icons blur when drawn
  large.
- The window and the mini bar sit on whole screen pixels, and are sized in whole pixels, or every
  line and letter in them blurs. Its dots are drawn from the circle art itself, tinted, without pixel
  snapping, which would leave small circles jagged.
- Text has a soft drop shadow, which reads sharper than flat text on the dark cards.
- Forever's spellbook is `PlayerSpellsFrame.SpellBookFrame` in the load-on-demand `Blizzard_PlayerSpells`, so the
  Spellbook Tab hooks it once that addon loads, and only after the switch is first turned on. It only post-hooks
  the book's OnShow and OnHide: its tab and panel are parented to UIParent and anchored to the book, never put
  inside it, and are drawn a frame later from a timer, outside the game's own show. Nothing of the book's is
  called, changed or hidden: it casts from its own buttons' clicks, which must stay untainted.
- The book has no secure frames on Forever, so the tab and panel show in combat too.
- Under Forever's Gamepad interface style (`ns.GamepadStandDown()`) the Spellbook Tab and the Panel at
  the Trainer stay off. That style opens and navigates the spellbook and the trainer from secure
  code, and our OnShow hooks on them would run inside it and taint it.
- The tab sits where the game anchors its own bottom tabs (`TAB_X`, `TAB_Y`: 22 in, 2 up); Forever hides those,
  as its book and talents open as separate windows.

## Checking

- `luacheck NaowhForever_Training` from the repo root.
- `lua Tools/regression/test-training.lua`: the plan and the trainer scan (Training.lua, Plan.lua,
  Trainer.lua), and what a rank adds (Upgrades.lua).
- `lua Tools/regression/test-training-builds.lua`: builds (Builds.lua) and every shipped tree.
- `lua Tools/regression/test-training-waypoint.lua`: the nearest trainer and the route (Route.lua).
- `lua Tools/regression/test-training-spellbook.lua`: the Spellbook Tab (UI/Spellbook.lua): off and free by default,
  what it lists, and showing and hiding with the book.
- `lua Tools/regression/test-training-window.lua`: every file Training.xml lists, the window opened
  and every part of it clicked.
